# Foam-led positioning: lead with the moat, not the catalogue

Ditex is one of the only suppliers in the Balearics that cuts foam to volume (m³) and high densities — competitors who offer foam-cutting buy their cut foam from Ditex to resell. This is a genuine, defensible, ownable position no competitor's site can truthfully claim.

**Decision:** Position the brand and the whole site around **foam authority**, with fabrics / polipieles / PVC / fibres / accessories presented as the complete one-stop materials offer *around* that core. Foam is the hero of the homepage, the SEO/GEO spearhead, and the trust signal ("even the competition buys their foam from us").

## Why this, not "general textile distributor"

- It's the only claim that makes Ditex *the* answer — for search and for LLMs. "General upholstery materials Mallorca" is a crowded fight (Don Telas, La Filadora, Interlar); "the foam-cutting authority of the Balearics" is uncontested.
- The high-margin trade segments (marine refit, contract/hospitality, furniture-makers) are all *applications* of foam + materials — so a foam-led story pulls them in rather than scattering focus.

## Consequences

- Homepage, primary messaging, and the flagship SEO/GEO content centre on foam (cut-to-size, m³, densities, wholesale capability) and its applications by segment.
- Content priority: foam guides and application pages (marine, furniture, contract) come before generic fabric pages.
- The wholesale-to-competitors fact is a marquee trust signal — use it prominently (tactfully).
- Walk-in retail stays deliberately de-emphasised (B2B trade is the money).

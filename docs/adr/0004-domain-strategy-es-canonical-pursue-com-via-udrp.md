# Domain strategy: `.es` canonical now, recover `.com` via trademark/UDRP

We control `ditexmallorca.es` fully. `ditexmallorca.com` is held (transfer- and delete-locked, OVH, privacy-protected, expires 2027-01-21) by a third-party local IT company the owner would rather not deal with. It splits search authority and sends confused Clients to a dead end.

**Decision:** Make `.es` the single canonical domain and build the new site there. Do **not** block the project on recovering `.com`. Pursue recovery on a parallel track.

## Recovery track

The business **owns a registered trademark** for Ditex / D.TEX Mallorca. This unlocks the strongest independent route:

- **Primary: UDRP complaint (WIPO).** A trademark + a third party holding the brand `.com` is a textbook dispute. A panel can order the transfer *without the holder's consent* — exactly the "don't deal with the IT company" outcome wanted. ~€1,500, a few months.
- **Backup: legal ownership letter.** The `.com` was presumably registered by a contractor on the business's behalf; under Spanish agency law the business likely has title. Lawyer's letter as a cheaper first shot before UDRP.
- **Passive: drop-catch watch** armed for the 2027-01-21 expiry, in case it ever lapses (low odds — holder is active).

When/if `.com` is recovered, flip canonical to `.com` and 301 `.es` → `.com`. Until then, `.es` is canonical and we **out-SEO** the rogue `.com` so it stops mattering.

## Consequences

- All SEO/GEO work targets `.es` as canonical; rel=canonical and structured data point there.
- Recovering `.com` later is a clean DNS/301 flip, not a rebuild — the site is domain-agnostic in config.
- UDRP is the owner's decision to fund/file; the website project proceeds regardless of its outcome.

# Languages: ship ES + CA + EN; defer German and French

The site launches in **Spanish, Catalan, and English**. German and French are deferred, not rejected.

## Reasoning

- **Spanish + Catalan** — the core Client base speaks them; non-negotiable.
- **English** — justified by the marine/nautical growth wedge and international buyers in Mallorca, and critically the business *can service it*: the user's partner speaks English (and French).
- **German — deferred despite the opportunity.** There is real money in Mallorca's German-speaking marine/property world, but (a) the business has no current foothold or connection there, and (b) **no one on staff speaks German.** Generating German-language leads nobody can answer would damage trust, not build it. Revisit when there is capacity to service it.
- **French — deferred.** The partner speaks it, but there is no identified French market to justify the translation/maintenance cost yet.

## Key principle

**Only offer a language the business can actually serve.** A lead in a language no one can reply to is worse than no lead. Language scope follows service capacity, not just market size.

## Consequences

- i18n is built to scale to more locales (adding DE/FR later is config + translation, not rework).
- The marine/GEO content is authored ES + EN first.
- Revisit German specifically if/when the business builds German-speaking service capacity.
